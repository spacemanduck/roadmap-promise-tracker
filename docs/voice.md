# Voice

The site is a receipts dataset presented without commentary.

- **State, don't characterise.** "Promised February 2026, shipped 4 February 2026" — not "finally shipped", not "on schedule as usual".
- **Every claim has a link.** If there is no archive link or patch note behind a sentence, the sentence does not belong on the site.
- **Say what is not known.** "No stated window" and "unverified draft" are shown, never hidden.
- **The publisher's words are quoted, ours are plain.** `title_as_stated` and `window_as_stated` are verbatim; our own copy is short declarative sentences.
- **Numbers carry their denominator.** Never "80% on time"; always "8 of 10 dated, delivered items on time".
- **No adjectives about publishers, no verbs of intent.** Not "quietly dropped", not "chose to delay". "Absent from the last two roadmaps" is a fact.
- **British spelling.** Normalised, colour, programme.
- Headings are nouns. Buttons are verbs.
